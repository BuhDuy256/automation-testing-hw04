## 5. Conclusion — When should AI be used (or not)?

Write 80–150 words describing patterns you observed. Where did AI shine? Where did AI fail? What is your recommendation for using AI in this kind of work in the future?

Across twenty-four audited interactions, AI was strongest when constrained by repository and runtime evidence: it preserved workflow correlations, implemented reviewed workloads, and retained attributable raw, report, resource, and GUI evidence. Human review remained essential.
